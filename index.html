Frequently Asked Questions

How does the missed-call automation work?

When someone calls your business number and you are unable to answer, our system immediately sends a personalized SMS on your behalf within 5 seconds to retain the lead before they call a competitor.

How do I receive Google 5-star reviews automatically?

You paste your official Google Business review link into your portal. After completing a job, TriDash automatically dispatches an SMS review invitation to your client.

How does the AI respond to reviews without sounding like a robot?

Our system uses tailored prompts to write natural, conversational replies. For 5-star reviews, it thanks the customer like a real person. For 1-star reviews, it uses de-escalation language to apologize and take the conversation offline.

Are there any long-term contracts or setup fees?

None at all. TriDash AI starts with a $10 30-day pilot on the Standard tier or $39/month for Pro AI, with zero setup fees. You can pause or cancel your subscription whenever you wish.
