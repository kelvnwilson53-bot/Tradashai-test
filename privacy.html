Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Effective Immediately

1. Information We Collect

When you register for a 7-day free trial or subscribe to TriDashai.com, we collect the personal and business details required to fulfill our service, including your name, email address, phone number, and business name. For initial trial registration, no payment card details are collected. Payment details are processed securely via our PCI-compliant Merchant of Record (Lemon Squeezy) only when you choose to activate a paid subscription.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information strictly to manage your account, automate transactional SMS responses to missed callers on your business line, dispatch review request messages to your clients, and deliver AI-assisted review management services.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

TriDashai.com does not sell, rent, or trade subscriber or end-customer personal data to third parties for marketing purposes. Data is shared exclusively with necessary service infrastructure partners (such as telecommunication carriers for SMS delivery and encrypted payment processors) solely to operate the platform.

4. Data Security

We implement 256-bit SSL encryption across all web pages and maintain industry-standard administrative and technical safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

5. Your Data Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or complete deletion of your personal account data at any time by contacting our support team or canceling your account subscription.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out through TriDashai.com or via support@tridashai.com.
