How completion and review requests will work

  1. Your receptionist records the enquiry and passes the details to you.
  2. After you have done the work, you confirm that it is complete. You can do this from the dashboard or by a simple text message.
  3. Only after your confirmation will TriDashAI send the customer your Google review link.

A call or enquiry is never treated as a completed job automatically. No review request is sent without your confirmation.

TriDashAI support

Help & FAQs

Simple answers to common questions about using TriDashAI.

What's the difference between my business number and my TriDashAI number?＋

Your business number is the number customers already know and call. Your dedicated TriDashAI number is the number that receives calls forwarded from it and connects callers to your receptionist. The TriDashAI number is assigned during activation.

Can I switch call forwarding on and off?＋

Call forwarding controls will be available after your number and phone-provider routing have been configured and tested. The exact activation and cancellation method depends on your phone provider. Do not assume the dashboard switch has changed your phone routing until the change is confirmed.

Does the receptionist book appointments automatically?＋

Appointment confirmation depends on Google Calendar being connected and availability being checked. Until that connection is active, the receptionist should not promise that an appointment has been booked.

Can the receptionist give customers a job price?＋

Your receptionist can explain the call-out charges you enter in your business settings. Your receptionist will only quote a fixed job price when you have supplied that price and its conditions. You can assess and price all other work yourself.

How are SMS and voice usage counted?＋

Voice usage is measured in minutes. SMS usage is counted by message segments, including both incoming and outgoing segments. Longer messages can use more than one segment.

What happens if I need more usage?＋

If extra usage bundles are available on your plan, you can review them using the Extra Bundles area in your dashboard. Check the bundle allowance and expiry details before purchasing.
